# Weekly Shopping:
Onions: 1.2 kg (about 8 large; versatile, buy in bulk bag for $2 savings)
Carrots: 600 g (about 6 medium; cheap staple, sub frozen if cheaper)
Potatoes: 2.1 kg (about 15 medium; buy in bulk bag to save $1-2)
Tomatoes: 150 g (about 1.5 medium)
Lettuce: 1 small head (for leaves and optional side; sub cabbage for longevity)
Optional: 2 green onion stalks (cheap garnish; skip to save)
Frozen mixed veggies: 225 g (peas/carrots; buy large bag for future use)
Boneless chicken thighs: 900 g (buy value pack; freeze extras if bulk deal)
Beef mince (80/20): 1.35 kg (bulk pack for $3-4 savings; 80/20 keeps it juicy cheaply)
Eggs: 9 (medium carton; versatile for breakfast too)
Milk: 750 ml (smallest carton; sub plant-based if preferred)
Cheese (grated or block): 450 g (store-brand cheddar; grate yourself to save)
Butter: 50 g (small stick; sub oil if cheaper)
Optional: Sour cream: 200 g (small tub for creaminess in 2 meals; skip for $1 savings)
White rice: 450 g uncooked (bulk bag; lasts months)
Flour: 225 g (small bag or bulk bin)
Spaghetti: 300 g (half pack; cheap generic)
Tinned tomatoes: 800 g (2 cans; generic brand under $1 each)
Soy sauce: 100 ml (small bottle; lasts forever, sub low-sodium)
Vegetable oil: 150 ml (small bottle if needed; multipurpose)
Beef stock: 150 ml (or 1-2 cubes; cheap bouillon powder)
Optional: Tomato paste: 2 tbsp (from tube; sub extra tinned tomatoes to save)
Paprika: 1 tbsp (small jar; sub chili powder if on hand)
Italian herbs: 1 tbsp (small jar; dried basil/oregano mix works)
Garlic powder: 1 tbsp (optional; sub fresh garlic bulb for $0.50)
Baking powder: 2 tsp (small tin; lasts ages)
Burger buns: 3 (or sub bread loaf slices to save $1)
Optional: Bread loaf: 1 small (if subbing for buns)
