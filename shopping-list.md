# Weekly Shopping List

## Fresh Produce
- **Onions**: 1.2 kg (about 8 large; versatile, buy in bulk bag for ~$2 savings)
- **Carrots**: 600 g (about 6 medium; cheap staple, sub frozen if cheaper)
- **Potatoes**: 2.1 kg (about 15 medium; buy in bulk bag to save $1–2)
- **Tomatoes**: 150 g (about 1–2 medium)
- **Lettuce**: 1 small head (for leaves and optional side; sub cabbage for longevity)
- **Green onions (optional)**: 2 stalks (cheap garnish; skip to save)

## Frozen
- **Frozen mixed veggies**: 225 g (peas/carrots; buy large bag for future use)

## Meat & Dairy
- **Boneless chicken thighs**: 900 g (value pack; freeze extras if bought in bulk)
- **Beef mince (80/20)**: 1.35 kg (bulk pack for $3–4 savings; juicier and cheaper)
- **Eggs**: 9 (medium carton; versatile)
- **Milk**: 750 ml (smallest carton; sub plant-based if preferred)
- **Cheese (grated or block)**: 450 g (store-brand cheddar; grate yourself to save)
- **Butter**: 50 g (small stick; sub oil if cheaper)
- **Sour cream (optional)**: 200 g (small tub; skip to save ~$1)

## Pantry & Dry Goods
- **White rice**: 450 g uncooked (bulk bag; lasts months)
- **Flour**: 225 g (small bag or bulk bin)
- **Spaghetti**: 300 g (half pack; generic brand)
- **Tinned tomatoes**: 800 g (2 cans; generic under $1 each)
- **Soy sauce**: 100 ml (small bottle; lasts a long time)
- **Vegetable oil**: 150 ml (small bottle if needed)
- **Beef stock**: 150 ml (or 1–2 cubes / bouillon powder)
- **Tomato paste (optional)**: 2 tbsp (from tube; sub extra tinned tomatoes)
- **Paprika**: 1 tbsp (sub chili powder if needed)
- **Italian herbs**: 1 tbsp (dried basil/oregano mix)
- **Garlic powder (optional)**: 1 tbsp (or sub fresh garlic bulb)
- **Baking powder**: 2 tsp (small tin; lasts ages)

## Bakery
- **Burger buns**: 3 (or sub bread slices to save ~$1)
- **Bread loaf (optional)**: 1 small (if subbing for buns)
