# 7-Day Dinner Plan (Markdown Version)

## Monday – Quick Chicken & Veg Stir-Fry with Rice
**Total time:** 30 min (prep 10 min + cook 20 min)  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

**Description:**  
This fast, one-pan stir-fry bursts with savory soy flavors and crisp veggies, using affordable chicken and staples. It's a light, nutritious start to the week that's cheap (under $7 total) and clears out basic produce for minimal shopping.

### Ingredients (3 people)
- 450 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into 2 cm pieces  
- 225 g uncooked white rice  
- 3 medium carrots (300 g), thinly sliced  
- 1.5 large onions (225 g), sliced  
- 3 tbsp soy sauce  
- 1.5 tbsp vegetable oil  
- 1.5 tsp garlic powder (or 3 fresh cloves)  
- Salt and black pepper, to taste  
- Optional: sliced green onion (1.5 stalks)

### Instructions
1. Rinse rice and cook in 450 ml water. Bring to a boil, then simmer covered for 15 min. Set aside.
2. Heat oil in a wok over medium-high. Add chicken, season, and stir-fry 6–8 min until cooked through. Remove.
3. Stir-fry carrots and onion 5–6 min. Return chicken, add soy sauce and garlic, toss 2–3 min.

**Pro tip:** Add a splash of water instead of extra oil if the pan dries out.  
**Calories (approx):** 480 kcal per serving

## Tuesday – Homemade Beef Burgers with Oven Fries
**Total time:** 40 min  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

### Ingredients
- 450 g beef mince (80/20)  
- 3 medium potatoes (600 g), wedges  
- 3 burger buns or bread slices  
- 1.5 small onions (150 g), sliced  
- 1.5 tomatoes (150 g), sliced  
- 3 lettuce leaves  
- 1.5 tbsp vegetable oil  
- 1.5 tsp paprika  
- Salt & pepper  
- Optional: 75 g grated cheese  

### Instructions
1. Bake seasoned potato wedges at 220°C for 25–30 min.
2. Mix mince with seasoning and onion. Form 3 patties.
3. Pan-fry patties 4–5 min per side. Add cheese in last minute.

**Calories:** ~550 kcal per serving

## Wednesday – Creamy Chicken Potato Bake
**Total time:** 45 min  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

### Ingredients
- 450 g chicken thighs  
- 6 medium potatoes (750 g), sliced  
- 1.5 large onions (225 g)  
- 450 ml milk  
- 150 g grated cheese  
- 1.5 tbsp butter  
- 1.5 tsp Italian herbs  
- Salt & pepper  

### Instructions
1. Layer potatoes, onion, and chicken in a greased dish.
2. Pour milk, top with cheese, bake at 200°C for 30–35 min.

**Calories:** ~520 kcal per serving

## Thursday – Classic Spaghetti Bolognese
**Total time:** 35 min  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

### Ingredients
- 450 g beef mince  
- 300 g spaghetti  
- 1.5 onions (150 g), chopped  
- 1.5 carrots (150 g), grated  
- 600 g tinned tomatoes  
- 1.5 tbsp tomato paste (optional)  
- 1.5 tsp Italian herbs  
- 1.5 tbsp oil  

### Instructions
1. Cook onion and carrot in oil.
2. Brown mince, add tomatoes and herbs, simmer 15 min.
3. Boil pasta, toss with sauce.

**Calories:** ~580 kcal per serving  

---

## Friday – Veggie Egg Fried Rice
**Total time:** 20 min  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

### Ingredients
- 450 g cooked rice  
- 6 eggs  
- 225 g frozen veggies  
- 1.5 onions (150 g)  
- 3 tbsp soy sauce  
- 1.5 tbsp oil  

### Instructions
1. Stir-fry onion and veggies.
2. Scramble eggs, add rice and soy, fry until hot.

**Calories:** ~420 kcal per serving

## Saturday – Beef-Stuffed Baked Potatoes
**Total time:** 50 min  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

### Ingredients
- 6 medium potatoes (750 g)  
- 450 g beef mince  
- 1.5 onions (150 g)  
- 150 ml beef stock  
- 1.5 tsp paprika  
- 75 g grated cheese  
- 1.5 tbsp butter  

### Instructions
1. Bake potatoes at 200°C for 40 min.
2. Cook mince with onion and seasoning.
3. Stuff potatoes, top with cheese, broil 2 min.

**Calories:** ~540 kcal per serving

## Sunday – Cheesy Vegetable Pancakes
**Total time:** 25 min  
**Difficulty:** Easy  

### Ingredients
- 225 g flour  
- 300 ml milk  
- 3 eggs  
- 150 g grated cheese  
- 150 g carrot, grated  
- 150 g onion, chopped  
- 1.5 tsp baking powder  
- 1.5 tbsp oil  

### Instructions
1. Mix batter with all ingredients.
2. Pan-fry pancakes 2–3 min per side.

**Calories:** ~450 kcal per serving
